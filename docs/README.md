# OctoAcme Project Management Documentation

This folder contains OctoAcme’s project management process documentation and is intended to be the starting point for onboarding and day-to-day reference. The process follows a lightweight but structured lifecycle: initiation, planning, execution and tracking, release, and retrospective improvement. Across these phases, teams focus on customer value, iterative delivery, clear ownership, and measurable outcomes.

Projects start with initiation to confirm the business need, align stakeholders, define success metrics, and make a go/no-go decision. Approved work moves into planning, where the team creates a prioritized backlog, defines acceptance criteria and Definition of Done, estimates scope, maps dependencies, and aligns on milestones and release timing. This workflow creates predictable delivery while keeping teams flexible enough to adapt.

Roles and personas are clearly defined to support cross-functional collaboration. Project Managers coordinate timelines, risk management, and stakeholder communication; Product Managers define problems, prioritize roadmaps, and measure impact; and Developers and QA/testing contributors implement and validate solutions against agreed acceptance criteria. Stakeholders stay engaged through regular checkpoints and approvals so delivery remains aligned with business priorities.

Communication and quality assurance are integrated into execution rather than treated as afterthoughts. Teams use recurring standups, weekly syncs, demos, and milestone updates, with documented escalation paths for blockers and incidents. Quality practices include unit and integration testing, smoke testing for critical flows, CI checks (including linting and security scanning), manual QA when needed, and release readiness checks with rollback planning. Retrospectives then convert lessons learned into tracked improvements for future work.
